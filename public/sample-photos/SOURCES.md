# Sample camera photos

These freely licensed photos stand in for the ESP32-CAM in this frontend-only demo. The demo labels them "Sample photo", never "LIVE CAMERA".
To use your own camera photos instead, put JPG files in the `camera-photos` folder at the top of the project.

- 01-stilt-roots.jpg: "Mangrove Stilt Roots (Rhizophora sp.) (8441001082).jpg" by Bernard DUPONT, CC BY-SA 2.0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangrove_Stilt_Roots_(Rhizophora_sp.)_(8441001082).jpg. Cropped to 4:3 and resized to 1024 x 768.
- 02-roots-at-the-water.jpg: "Close view of red mangrove roots.jpg" by Steve Hillebrand, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Public domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_view_of_red_mangrove_roots.jpg. Cropped to 4:3 and resized to 1024 x 768.
- 03-rhizophora-leaves.jpg: "Rhizophora apiculata Blume (52263667127).jpg" by Dinesh Valke from Thane, India, CC BY-SA 2.0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rhizophora_apiculata_Blume_(52263667127).jpg. Cropped to 4:3 and resized to 1024 x 768.
- 04-mangrove-trees-malaysia.jpg: "Mangrove trees Malaysia.jpg" by Boring npc, CC BY-SA 4.0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangrove_trees_Malaysia.jpg. Cropped to 4:3 and resized to 1024 x 768.
